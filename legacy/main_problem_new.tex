For \(n\ge 2\) and \(0<a<1\), consider the real nonnormal tridiagonal Toeplitz matrix family
\[
A_n(a)\coloneqq \operatorname{tridiag}(a,0,1)
=
\begin{bmatrix}
0 & 1 \\
a & 0 & \ddots \\
& \ddots & \ddots & \ddots \\
&& \ddots & 0 & 1 \\
&&& a & 0
\end{bmatrix}\in \mathbb{R}^{n\times n}.
\]
For \(\varepsilon>0\), its \(\varepsilon\)-pseudospectrum is
\[
\sigma_\varepsilon\bigl(A_n(a)\bigr)
\coloneqq
\left\{
z\in\mathbb C\colon
\bigl\|(z I_n-A_n(a))^{-1}\bigr\|_2>\varepsilon^{-1}
\right\},
\]
with the usual convention that the resolvent norm is \(+\infty\) on the spectrum \(\sigma\bigl(A_n(a)\bigr)\).
Equivalently,
\[
\sigma_\varepsilon\bigl(A_n(a)\bigr)
=
\left\{
z\in\mathbb C\colon
s_{\min}\bigl(z I_n-A_n(a)\bigr)<\varepsilon
\right\}.
\]
Define eventual connectedness threshold
\[
N_e(a, \varepsilon)\coloneqq\min \left\{N \geq 2\colon \text{for any } n \geq N, \sigma_\varepsilon\bigl(A_n(a)\bigr) \text{ is connected}\right\}.
\]
and first-hit connectedness threshold
\[
N_f(a, \varepsilon)\coloneqq\min \left\{N \geq 2\colon \sigma_\varepsilon\bigl(A_N(a)\bigr) \text{ is connected}\right\}.
\]
Then, with fixed \(a\) and \(\varepsilon\), \(N_e(a, \varepsilon)\) always equals \(N_f(a, \varepsilon)\).
Furthermore, if \(\sigma_\varepsilon\bigl(A_n(a)\bigr)\) is connected, it is simply connected.
Lastly, let \(N_c(a, \varepsilon)\coloneqq N_e(a, \varepsilon)=N_f(a, \varepsilon)\) denote the common connectedness threshold.
Give sharp lower and upper bounds for \(N_c(a, \varepsilon)\) in terms of \(a\) and \(\varepsilon\).
