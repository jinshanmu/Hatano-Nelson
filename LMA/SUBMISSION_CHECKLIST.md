# LMA submission checklist

## Files and manuscript

- [ ] Open the final PDF and check the title, author details, abstract,
  keywords, MSC codes, figures, references, and declarations.
- [ ] Confirm that the source archive compiles without internet access.
- [ ] Upload the manuscript PDF and source archive; upload separate figure
  files only if the live portal requests them.
- [ ] Preview the portal-generated PDF before approving the submission.

## Portal metadata

- [ ] Copy fields from `SUBMISSION_METADATA.md` and compare them character by
  character with the manuscript.
- [ ] Select Research Article and the closest matrix-analysis/numerical-linear-
  algebra subject classifications offered by the portal.
- [ ] Confirm the corresponding-author email and ORCID.
- [ ] Paste `COVER_LETTER_LMA.md` if a cover-letter field is available.

## Author confirmations

- [ ] Confirm originality, authorship, and that the manuscript is not under
  consideration elsewhere.
- [ ] Confirm funding and conflict-of-interest statements.
- [ ] Confirm that the GitHub repository is public and synchronized with the
  submitted mathematical results and figure code.
- [ ] Re-read the AI-assisted-preparation statement naming `OpenAI ChatGPT
  Work 5.6 Sol` and confirm it matches the tool used.
- [ ] Decide whether to choose optional open access; LMA is hybrid, and the
  standard non-open-access route has no APC according to the journal page.

## Live-guideline override

The live Taylor & Francis portal and current LMA instructions take precedence
over this checklist.  If they require the publisher's `interact` class,
convert only the preamble/front matter and retain the validated mathematical
body.
