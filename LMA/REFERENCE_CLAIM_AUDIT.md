# Claim-to-source citation audit

Audit completed on 2026-08-13 against the local full texts in
`../references/`.  The bibliography has 22 cited entries represented by 21
distinct PDF files: the 2025 Ammari et al. article and its 2026 corrigendum are
stored together in one 31-page PDF.

The standard used here is stricter than metadata or abstract matching.  For
every citation in the manuscript, the cited sentence was compared with a
specific passage, theorem, equation, or figure in the original source.  Page
numbers below are one-based pages in the local PDF; visible journal or book
pagination is added where useful.

## Outcome

- All 22 bibliography entries have direct original-source support for their
  revised manuscript use.
- Three broad group-citation sentences were split so that each source now
  supports a nearby, source-specific statement.
- The 1994 Boettcher citation is now described as a result for truncated
  Wiener--Hopf operators, rather than being folded into a generic claim about
  finite Toeplitz matrices.
- The 2025 tridiagonal `k`-Toeplitz paper is cited together with its 2026
  corrigendum.  The manuscript refers to the corrected finite-matrix
  pseudospectral limit and does not rely on the original erroneous Theorem 3.3
  or the affected versions of Theorems 3.5 and 3.7.
- The Kiorpelidis--Makris paper alone supports the order-driven merger claim;
  Ammari et al. support exponential edge localization, while Sirker supports
  the distinction between nonnormal spectral instability, the skin effect,
  and stable topology.
- No central Toeplitz threshold or strict-monotonicity proof imports a theorem
  from the positioning literature.  The standard component lemma is cited to
  two exact sources and is also proved in the manuscript.

## Citation-dependent statements inside the proof

Only two standard facts in the mathematical development carry literature
citations:

1. The perturbation and resolvent/least-singular-value definitions of the
   pseudospectrum, supported by `TrefethenEmbree2005`, Definition (2.3).
2. The fact that every strict pseudospectral component contains an
   eigenvalue, supported verbatim by `BurkeLewisOverton2003`, Theorem 5.1,
   and by `TrefethenEmbree2005`, Theorem 2.4(i).  The manuscript supplies its
   own proof immediately after the citation.

All remaining citations position the contribution or interpret the final
Hatano--Nelson specialization.  None is used as an unverified premise in the
exact threshold or all-order strict-monotonicity arguments.
